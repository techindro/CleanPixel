Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CleanPixel AI inpainting and security

How does the AI inpainting technology reconstruct backgrounds?

CleanPixel uses advanced Fast-Marching and Navier-Stokes differential partial equations combined with deep neural texture synthesis. It samples surrounding pixels, lighting vectors, and film grain to synthesize a clean, natural background without blur seams.

Is my uploaded media stored or used to train AI models?

No. We respect your copyright and privacy. Your images, PDFs, and video files are processed in ephemeral memory and automatically purged immediately after download. We never train public AI models on user assets.

What file formats and resolutions are supported?

CleanPixel natively supports PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, animated GIF, SVG, BMP, TIFF, and multi-page PDF documents. Video mode supports MP4, WebM, and MOV formats up to 4K UHD resolution.

Can I use CleanPixel for commercial e-commerce images?

Yes! CleanPixel is widely used by Amazon and Shopify store owners to clean supplier watermarks and prepare high-converting, compliant product catalogs.
